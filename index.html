Family Agent

A private household assistant for one family.

Family Agent is a small, self-hosted program that helps one family keep its shared calendar and inbox in order. It runs on a computer in that family's home and talks to the family through a group text thread. It is not a product, has no customers, and is not available to anyone else.

It connects to a single Google account that the family owns, so it can read the family calendar and inbox, add and update calendar events, and save draft email replies for a family member to review and send.

Read the privacy policy for what it accesses and how that information is handled.
