Privacy Policy

Family Agent · last updated October 19, 2026

Family Agent is a private program used by one household. It is operated by that household, for that household, and no one else can sign up for or use it. This page explains what Google account data it accesses and what happens to that data.

What it accesses

With the family's permission, Family Agent uses Google's APIs to:

  • read and update events on the family's Google Calendar;
  • read email in the family's Gmail inbox;
  • create draft email messages in that inbox for a family member to review and send.

It does not send email on its own, and it does not access any Google account other than the family's own.

How the data is used

Calendar and email data is used only to answer the family's own requests and to keep the family calendar accurate, for example turning a school email into a calendar event. Nothing is used for advertising, analytics, profiling, or training.

Where the data lives

The program runs on a computer inside the family's home. Google data it reads is processed there and may be relayed to an AI model provider (Anthropic) in order to answer a request, under that provider's terms. It is not sold, shared with, or transferred to any other third party.

Retention and deletion

Access and refresh tokens are stored only on the home computer. The family can revoke the program's access at any time from their Google Account's third-party access settings, which immediately prevents any further access. Any locally stored data can be deleted by the family at will.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Family Agent's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
